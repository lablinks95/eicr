What the rules require

The four dates that matter to a landlord

The Electrical Safety Standards in the Private Rented Sector (England) Regulations 2020 set out what has to happen and when. Most enforcement cases come down to a missed deadline rather than a failed inspection.

Councils can impose a financial penalty of up to £40,000 where a landlord is in breach of these duties. The maximum rose from £30,000 in the 2025 amendment regulations.

  • 5 YEARS

    Inspection interval

    The installation must be inspected and tested by a qualified person at least every five years, or more often if the report says so.

  • 28 DAYS

    Report to tenants

    A copy goes to existing tenants within 28 days of the inspection, and to a new tenant before they move in.

  • 28 DAYS

    Remedial work

    Where the report is unsatisfactory, the work must be completed within 28 days — or sooner, if the report specifies a shorter period — with written confirmation obtained.

  • 7 DAYS

    Council request

    If the local authority asks for the report, you have seven days to supply it.

Reading your report

What the codes on an EICR mean

Every observation on the report carries a classification code. Those codes decide whether the installation is recorded as satisfactory, and whether a 28-day clock starts.

C1

Danger present

Risk of injury. Made safe on the day where possible, and you are told before the inspector leaves.

C2

Potentially dangerous

Remedial work required. The report is unsatisfactory until it is put right.

C3

Improvement recommended

Advisory only. A C3 on its own does not make the report unsatisfactory and carries no deadline.

FI

Further investigation

Something could not be resolved during the inspection. Treated the same as a C2 for compliance purposes.

FAQ

Questions landlords and agents ask

What is an EICR certificate?

An Electrical Installation Condition Report is a formal inspection and test of the fixed electrical installation in a property — the consumer unit, circuits, sockets, switches and fixed accessories. The report records the condition of the installation, lists any observations with a classification code, and states whether the installation is satisfactory or unsatisfactory for continued use.

Who needs an EICR?

Landlords of rented homes in England must have the electrical installation inspected and tested by a qualified person at least every five years, and provide the report to tenants and to the local council on request. Letting agents and property managers usually arrange this on the landlord's behalf.

Owner-occupiers are not required to hold one, though many arrange an inspection before selling or after buying.

How long does an inspection take?

A one or two-bedroom flat usually takes around two to three hours. Larger houses, properties with several consumer units, and HMOs take longer. Power is switched off for parts of the test, so tenants should be told in advance.

When will I receive my certificate?

The digital certificate is issued as a PDF within 24 hours of the site visit. If the inspector finds anything dangerous, you are told on the day rather than waiting for the paperwork.

How much does an EICR cost?

Guide prices start from £129 for a one-bedroom flat. The final price depends on the size of the property, the number of circuits and consumer units, and whether it is let as an HMO. You receive a fixed price in writing before anything is booked.

Which areas do you cover?

All 32 London boroughs and the City of London, including Croydon, Barnet, Ealing, Lambeth, Newham, Hackney, Wandsworth and Tower Hamlets.

What happens if my property fails the EICR?

An unsatisfactory result means the report contains a C1, C2 or FI observation. Landlords in England must complete the remedial work within 28 days of the report, or sooner if the report specifies, and obtain written confirmation that the work has been done. You receive a plain-English summary of what needs attention.

Do you carry out the remedial work as well?

Inspection and reporting is the core service. Where remedial work is needed we can quote for it separately, so you are free to use your own contractor if you prefer.
